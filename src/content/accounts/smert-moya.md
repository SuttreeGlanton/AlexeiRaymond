---
title: Смерть моя
fullText: true
---

<p>
Never seen though<br>
Felt nesting ’midst bonebranches &amp; bloodvines—<br>
Blackplumed, you must be<br>
held back.<br><br>
I’ll die?<br>
<em>Aye.</em><br><br>
Your mimicry; mocking? Morose?<br>
It is heard and reckoned<br>
As you thrash to burst free<br>
Past brood beat blood and bone.<br>
Out.<br><br>
And then where?
</p>
